## Shared direction

High-fidelity landscape concept boards for a Pakistan multi-sport venue booking Android app. Each board shows two front-on mobile screens: discovery/home and court reservation. Include readable sport selection, court imagery, date and time selection, PKR pricing and a prominent reservation action. Use genuinely distinct composition, typography and imagery rather than recoloring a common dashboard. Preserve large touch targets and clear controls.
